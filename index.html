Privacy Policy

Vasu Studio respects your privacy. Our policy is transparent: we prioritize your security above all else.

verified_user Data Safety

visibilityWhat Data Do We Collect?

We do not collect any personal, sensitive user data (such as your legal name, email address, or contact details) without your explicit consent. Our verified partner platforms process only anonymous advertising identifiers (Ad IDs) and core system specifications, including device hardware type and operating system version.

settingsWhy & How We Use It?

  • Seamless Gameplay: Device-local storage parameters are utilized to securely maintain your custom configurations, local achievements, and match progress.
  • Non-Intrusive Monetization: Integrated mediation channels (Google AdMob and ironSource LevelPlay) leverage anonymous tokens exclusively to serve compliant, age-appropriate, and non-personalized advertising content.

shieldData Security & Safety

User safety is our absolute priority. We deploy strict memory-safe runtime execution boundaries and data isolation rules to preserve a safe in-game ecosystem. We strictly adhere to zero-data-broker policies and never sell, trade, or share user details with unverified entities.
